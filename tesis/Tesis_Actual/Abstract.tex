\chapter*{Abstract}

\vspace*{-8mm}

Physics-Informed Neural Networks with sinusoidal activation functions
(SIREN-PINNs) are evaluated for solving the Helmholtz equation in one and two
dimensions as a foundation for optical speckle simulation. With $\omega_0 = 1.0$
calibrated to the wavenumber, the SIREN architecture achieves relative $L^2$
errors of $0.006\%$ in 1D ($5 \times 64$ neurons) and $0.171\%$ in 2D
($5 \times 128$) against analytical solutions, three and nearly two orders of
magnitude below the $5\%$ thesis threshold.
The speckle extension uses a spatially correlated random-phase screen and a
semianalytical angular-spectrum reference, and is solved through a modal
reduction that imposes the Cauchy condition exactly within the architecture.
Across five independent phase realizations at $z=1\lambda$ the complex-field
$L^2$ error is $3.161\%$, dominated by the evanescent fraction of the spectrum
that the modal basis cannot represent; measured over the propagating subspace
the error is $0.042\%$. The ensemble contrast is $C=0.9489$, against a mean
reference ensemble contrast of $C=0.9661$ over 64 realizations each.
The validation extends to $z=2\lambda$ with a single-domain PINN-SIREN per
screen
($L^2=0.993\%$) and, through domain decomposition into $1\lambda$ local
networks coupled by hard Cauchy conditions, up to $z=5\lambda$ ($L^2=2.244\%$
at the end of the path and a maximum of $3.507\%$ across $201$ planes) and up
to $z=10\lambda$ with ten networks ($L^2=2.617\%$ and a maximum of $3.726\%$),
in all cases over five known phase realizations. A twenty-block chain to
$z=20\lambda$, tried on a single screen and without refinement, does not meet
the criterion ($L^2=8.743\%$).
Optimization protocols differ across experiments and should not be
generalized: the 1D and 2D validations use Adam followed by L-BFGS with Latin
Hypercube Sampling (LHS); the single-domain modal formulations at $z=1\lambda$
and $z=2\lambda$ use Adam only and no LHS, because their loss contains the modal
residual alone; and the $z=5\lambda$ chain trains or refines each block with
Adam followed by L-BFGS. Robustness to initialization was verified in the 2D case
with three independent seeds, yielding a component-wise mean $L^2$ error of
$0.192 \pm 0.089\%$ (population standard deviation); in the modal formulation the
five realizations vary the physical screen and not the network seed, so that
robustness is not characterized there.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} Physics-Informed Neural Networks, SIREN,
Helmholtz equation, optical speckle, sinusoidal activation, Latin Hypercube
Sampling.
