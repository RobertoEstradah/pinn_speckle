\subsection{1D Validation - Helmholtz with Analytical Solution}
\label{subsec:results1d}

Table~\ref{tab:results1d} shows the accuracy metrics obtained for the 1D Helmholtz
equation with the SIREN $5\times64$ architecture, $\omega_0 = 1.0$.

\begin{table}[t]
\centering
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.2}
\caption{accuracy metrics, PINN-SIREN 1D ($k = 2\pi$)}
\label{tab:results1d}
\begin{tabular}{lc}
\hline
Metric & Value \\
\hline
L2 error (\%)          & 0.006 \\
MSE                    & $1.5 \times 10^{-9}$ \\
RMSE                   & $3.9 \times 10^{-5}$ \\
MAE                    & $3.50 \times 10^{-5}$ \\
Maximum error          & $5.77 \times 10^{-5}$ \\
$R^2$                  & 1.000000 \\
Pearson correlation    & 1.000000 \\
\hline
Adam epochs            & 15{,}000 / 15{,}000 \\
L-BFGS evaluations     & 202 / 500 \\
Total time             & 131.1 s \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
\noindent\footnotesize Evaluated on a uniform grid of 1{,}000 points in $[0,1]$.
SIREN architecture: 5 layers $\times$ 64 neurons, $\omega_0 = 1.0$.
Analytical solution: $E_{\mathrm{exact}}(x) = \cos(kx)$.
GPU: NVIDIA RTX 5050, SEED = 42, laptop on AC power with no concurrent
processes. ``L-BFGS evaluations'' counts calls to the loss function during
the \textit{strong Wolfe} line search, not optimizer iterations. In a prior
run without these conditions the total time was 504.2 s: the same hardware,
under degraded thermal conditions, reproduced all accuracy metrics
identically at 60.4 ms/epoch versus 8.75 ms/epoch under controlled
conditions.
\normalsize

The L2 error of $0.006\%$ remains below the threshold adopted in this work ($< 5\%$) by
almost three orders of magnitude.
The fit is practically perfect: $R^2 = 1.000000$ and Pearson correlation
$= 1.000000$ over the entire evaluation grid.

\subsection{2D Validation - Helmholtz with Complex Field}
\label{subsec:results2d}

Table~\ref{tab:results2d} reports the metrics for the 2D validation with the
optimized SIREN $5\times128$ architecture, $\omega_0 = 1.0$, and LHS sampling with
3{,}000 points.

\begin{table}[t]
\centering
\footnotesize
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.2}
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{4pt}
\caption{accuracy metrics, PINN-SIREN 2D ($k = 2\pi$, $k_x = k_y = k/\sqrt{2}$)}
\label{tab:results2d}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lcc@{}}
\hline
Metric & $E_{\mathrm{real}}$ & $E_{\mathrm{imag}}$ \\
\hline
L2 error (\%)          & 0.214 & 0.127 \\
MSE                    & $2.20 \times 10^{-6}$ & $8.43 \times 10^{-7}$ \\
RMSE                   & $1.48 \times 10^{-3}$ & $9.18 \times 10^{-4}$ \\
MAE                    & $1.27 \times 10^{-3}$ & $7.71 \times 10^{-4}$ \\
Maximum error          & $3.05 \times 10^{-3}$ & $2.93 \times 10^{-3}$ \\
$R^2$                  & 0.999995 & 0.999998 \\
Pearson correlation    & 0.999998 & 0.999999 \\
\hline
Average L2 error (\%) & \multicolumn{2}{c}{0.171} \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
\noindent\footnotesize Evaluated on a uniform $100 \times 100$ grid.
Architecture: SIREN $5 \times 128$, $\omega_0 = 1.0$, 66{,}690 parameters.
Collocation points: LHS $N_c = 3{,}000$.
Boundary: plane wave $e^{i(k_x x + k_y y)}$ on 4 edges, $N_b = 300$ pts/edge.
Training: Adam (8{,}737 epochs) + L-BFGS (1{,}035 objective-function
evaluations), total time 209.9 s (laptop GPU on AC power, no concurrent
processes).
Dispersion across seeds (SEED $\in \{42, 123, 777\}$), measured in the
same session:
average $L^2$ error $= 0.192 \pm 0.089$\,\% (mean $\pm$ std.\ dev.).
\normalsize

The average L2 error of $0.171\%$ remains below the threshold adopted in this work
($< 5\%$) by more than one order of magnitude. Accuracy remains high in both
components of the complex field, with $R^2 > 0.9999$ for $E_{\mathrm{real}}$ and
$E_{\mathrm{imag}}$.

In a preliminary experiment with $d = 64$ (same $N_c$, $N_b$, and optimization
protocol) the average error was $0.436\%$, versus $0.171\%$ with $d = 128$.
This comparison also involves a difference in the number of L-BFGS
evaluations between the two runs, so the improvement cannot be attributed
in isolation to the increase in network capacity; it is reported as
qualitative evidence, not as a controlled ablation. No experiment is
available that isolates the contribution of LHS sampling relative to an
equivalent Cartesian grid.
